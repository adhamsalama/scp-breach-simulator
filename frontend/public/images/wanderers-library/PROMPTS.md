# The Wanderer's Library — Image Prompts

Style: **infinite impossible architecture, warm amber candlelight and cold blue magic, ancient wood and endless shelves, surreal and overwhelming. 16:9 landscape, photorealistic, no people.**

## grand-atrium.jpg
An impossibly vast atrium inside the Wanderer's Library — bookshelves stretching hundreds of meters upward into darkness, connected by spiraling staircases and rope bridges. Warm amber lantern light. Books floating gently through the air. The ceiling is invisible, lost in shadow. Ancient dark wood everywhere. Surreal, beautiful, and terrifying in scale. No people. Photorealistic fantasy.

## reading-rooms.jpg
A cozy but deeply strange reading alcove inside the Wanderer's Library — leather armchairs facing a crackling fireplace, books stacked on every surface, candles in iron holders. The shelves behind contain books in languages that don't exist. The window shows a view of endless more shelves rather than sky. No people. Photorealistic, warm and unsettling.

Warning symbols in dozens of languages cover the gate. The air beyond shimmers slightly. No people. Photorealistic, dark fantasy horror.

The shelves descend further than should be physically possible. Dust motes hang in the air. No people. Photorealistic, ancient and eerie.
