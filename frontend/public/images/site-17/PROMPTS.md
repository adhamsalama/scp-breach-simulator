# SCP Foundation Site-17 — Image Prompts

Style: **clinical white interiors, sterile humanoid containment, unsettling quietness. 16:9 landscape, photorealistic, no people.**

## humanoid-cells.jpg
A long corridor of small containment cells inside SCP Foundation Site-17, each sealed with heavy transparent reinforced doors. The cells are sparse — a bed, a drain, a camera. Sterile white walls with SCP classification plaques. Cold fluorescent lighting, one cell door hanging open. No people. Photorealistic, clinical horror.

## interview-room.jpg
A sparse SCP Foundation interview room: two chairs facing each other across a bolted metal table, a one-way mirror on the wall, recording equipment in the corner. Fluorescent tube lighting. A Foundation logo on the wall. One chair overturned. Signs of a struggle. No people. Photorealistic.

## observation-deck.jpg
A darkened SCP Foundation observation room with a wide reinforced glass window overlooking an empty containment chamber below. Banks of monitoring equipment, flickering screens. Emergency red lighting reflecting off the glass. No people. Photorealistic, cinematic.

## d-class-dormitory.jpg
A stark underground dormitory for D-Class personnel — rows of metal bunk beds bolted to the floor, orange jumpsuits draped over frames, personal items left behind. Flickering fluorescent lights. A SCP Foundation insignia above the sealed door. No people. Photorealistic, horror atmosphere.

## security-station.jpg
An abandoned SCP Foundation security station inside Site-17 — a bank of CCTV monitors showing empty corridors, a weapons rack with missing equipment, an overturned coffee mug. Emergency lighting. A breach alert blinking on the main screen. No people. Photorealistic.

## psych-ward.jpg
A clinical SCP Foundation psychological evaluation ward — white padded walls, restraint chairs with straps, a whiteboard with half-erased diagrams. Harsh overhead lighting casting sharp shadows. Filing cabinets overturned. Drawings scratched into the walls. No people. Photorealistic, deeply unsettling.

## decontamination-chamber.jpg
A cylindrical decontamination chamber inside SCP Foundation Site-17 — white tiled walls with shower heads and UV emitters, a heavy sealed door on either end. Hazmat warning symbols on the walls. Yellow chemical residue on the floor. Emergency lights. No people. Photorealistic.

## exit-airlock.jpg
A double-sealed airlock at the exit of SCP Foundation Site-17 — two massive blast doors with red warning lights, pressure gauges, and a biometric scanner. The inner door is open; the outer door shows a crack of outside light. Signs reading "AUTHORIZED EXIT — LEVEL 4 CLEARANCE". No people. Photorealistic, cinematic.
