# Penrose General Hospital — Image Prompts

Style: **abandoned hospital, clinical decay, peeling paint over sterile white, medical equipment left mid-procedure, horror. 16:9 landscape, photorealistic, no people.**

## emergency-room.jpg
An abandoned hospital emergency room — curtained bays with overturned gurneys, IV poles on the floor, scattered medical equipment. Harsh fluorescent lighting, some blown. Blood on the tile floor leading toward a corridor. Medical charts scattered. No people. Photorealistic, horror.

## surgery-theater.jpg
An abandoned hospital surgery theater — a central operating table under a massive circular surgical lamp still powered on, surgical tools laid out and untouched, monitors showing flatlines. Tiles cracked and stained. The sterile environment now deeply wrong. No people. Photorealistic, clinical horror.

## psychiatric-ward.jpg
An abandoned hospital psychiatric ward — a long corridor of rooms with small reinforced windows in the doors, padded walls visible inside some rooms. Restraints hanging from a bed frame. Handwriting covering the walls in marker. Flickering fluorescent lights. No people. Photorealistic, deeply unsettling.

## morgue.jpg
An abandoned hospital morgue — stainless steel examination tables, a wall of body storage drawers, some partially open and empty. Cold blue lighting. Drains in the tiled floor. A toe tag on the floor. No people. Photorealistic, cold horror.

## pharmacy.jpg
An abandoned hospital pharmacy — shelves of medicine bottles, some ransacked and emptied, others untouched. A dispensing counter with a shattered glass screen. Papers everywhere. Emergency lighting casting orange across white pill bottles. A Foundation hazard label over some shelves. No people. Photorealistic.

## hospital-rooftop.jpg
The rooftop of an abandoned hospital at night — a helicopter landing pad with faded markings, HVAC units, a rusted water tower. Foundation perimeter lights visible below surrounding the building. The city skyline dark and distant. No people. Photorealistic, cinematic.

## main-lobby.jpg
An abandoned hospital main lobby — a reception desk with overturned chairs, an information board with outdated notices, a Foundation quarantine notice taped over the entrance doors. Wheelchairs abandoned. One elevator door open and stuck. Emergency lighting. No people. Photorealistic, horror atmosphere.

## basement-tunnels.jpg
The underground utility tunnels beneath a hospital — concrete walls with bundles of pipes and cables, puddles of stagnant water on the floor, low ceiling, a long vanishing perspective. One work light still on far in the distance. Foundation warning tape across a side passage. No people. Photorealistic, claustrophobic horror.
