# The Black Forest Exclusion Zone — Image Prompts

Style: **dense dark forest, Foundation cordoned wilderness, fog, isolation, no escape. 16:9 landscape, photorealistic, no people.**

## deep-forest.jpg
The interior of a dense, ancient forest at night — massive dark tree trunks, no undergrowth, a carpet of dead leaves, thin beams of moonlight barely penetrating the canopy. Fog at ground level. Foundation warning tape visible on a distant tree, half torn. No paths. No light source nearby. No people. Photorealistic, horror.

## forest-clearing.jpg
A circular clearing in a dark forest — dead grass, a ring of trees surrounding the open space, moonlight illuminating the center. Strange symbols pressed into the earth in a pattern. Foundation equipment casing abandoned at the edge. An unnatural stillness. No people. Photorealistic, eerie.

## abandoned-cabin.jpg
A small wooden cabin deep in a dark forest — rotting walls, a collapsed porch, one window lit from inside with a faint orange glow. The door slightly ajar. Foundation survey markers in the ground nearby. Trees pressing close on all sides. No people. Photorealistic, horror atmosphere.

## riverbank.jpg
A dark riverbank inside the forest exclusion zone — black water moving slowly, fog on the surface, twisted tree roots along the bank. A Foundation inflatable boat deflated and abandoned on the shore. Moonlight reflecting on the water. No people. Photorealistic, eerie and isolated.

## cave-entrance.jpg
A cave entrance set into a rocky hillside deep in the forest — darkness beyond the threshold, wet stone, Foundation caution tape strung across the entrance (partially broken). A flashlight dropped just inside, still on. Trees looming overhead. No people. Photorealistic, horror.

## overgrown-road.jpg
An old road reclaimed by forest — cracked asphalt with grass and roots splitting through it, trees forming a canopy overhead, a rusted Foundation roadblock barrier across the road. Headlight beams from an abandoned vehicle in the distance. Fog. No people. Photorealistic, liminal horror.

## watchtower.jpg
A Foundation metal watchtower rising above the treeline in a dark forest — a ladder up the center, a small enclosed observation platform at the top with a single light on. The forest stretches endlessly in all directions below. No roads visible. No people. Photorealistic, cinematic.

## forest-perimeter.jpg
The perimeter of the Black Forest Exclusion Zone — a chain-link fence topped with razor wire, Foundation warning signs every few meters reading "EXCLUSION ZONE — AUTHORIZED PERSONNEL ONLY". Floodlights on poles. The forest dark and impenetrable beyond the fence. The outside world barely visible on the other side. No people. Photorealistic.
